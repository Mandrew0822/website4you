About

A small studio, making websites with care.

website4you is an independent website design studio. We build sleek, custom sites for small businesses and individuals — with a clear process, honest pricing, and no layers of account managers between you and the person doing the work.

Why we exist

Good websites should not be reserved for companies with five-figure budgets. Plenty of excellent businesses and talented people are stuck with a site that undersells them — or no site at all — because the options feel expensive, confusing, or slow.

We started website4you to make the middle path better: real custom design, a focused scope, a fair fixed price, and a launch timeline measured in days rather than months. No templates passed off as custom work. No mystery invoices. No subscription you cannot leave.

How we work

We keep the process deliberately simple. You send a brief, we ask a few useful questions, and you get a written scope with a fixed price. Once that is agreed, we design, build, refine, and hand over.

You work directly with the person designing and building your site. That means fewer misunderstandings, faster decisions, and a result that reflects the conversation you actually had.

What we believe

Custom, not recycled

Your site is designed around your content and customers, not a theme with your logo in the corner.

Plain language

No jargon, no inflated strategy decks. Clear explanations and clear decisions.

Fast by default

Lightweight static builds, optimized images, and clean code — because speed is part of quality.

Yours to keep

You own the files, the domain, and the design. No lock-in and no required subscription.

Who we work with

Our clients are small businesses, tradespeople, studios, restaurants, consultants, creatives, and independent professionals. Some need a first website. Some need a focused landing page for a campaign. Others have a site that works but looks tired. The common thread is a desire for something considered, without a bloated process.

We are not the right fit for large e-commerce catalogues, complex web applications, or projects that need a full brand strategy team. We will say so if that is the case — and point you in a better direction.

Say hello

If you have a project in mind, send a short note to customwebsiteforyou@gmail.com or use the project brief on the homepage. We reply within 24 hours, usually with questions and a clear next step.
