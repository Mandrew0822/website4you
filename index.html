FAQ

Good questions, answered.

Still curious about something? Ask us directly — we’re happy to talk it through.

How long does a website take?

Most one-page sites are ready in about a week. Multi-page business sites usually take one to two weeks, depending on content and feedback. We’ll give you a real timeline before we start.

Do I need to write the copy myself?

You can, or we can help shape it. Send rough notes about what you do and who it’s for, and we’ll turn them into clear, confident copy that fits the design.

What do you need from me to begin?

A short brief, any logo or brand assets you have, and a few sites you like the look of. That’s enough for us to get moving — we’ll ask for the rest as we go.

Will it work on phones and tablets?

Always. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested across screen sizes, so it looks just as sharp on a phone as it does on a desktop.

Who owns the finished website?

You do. The files, the domain, and the design are yours. There’s no subscription and no lock-in — we hand everything over when the project is complete.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% at handover. Everything is confirmed in writing first, so there are no surprises on either side.

Can you update the site after it’s live?

Yes. Small updates can be quoted separately, or we can show you how to edit the content yourself — whichever you prefer.
