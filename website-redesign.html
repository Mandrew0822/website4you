Signs your site is holding you back

Most outdated sites do not fail loudly. They quietly make visitors hesitate, squint, or leave. If any of these sound familiar, a redesign will probably pay for itself:

  • It looks visibly older than your competitors’ sites
  • It is difficult to use on a phone
  • The homepage loads slowly or jumps around as it loads
  • You cannot tell what the business does within a few seconds
  • Important information is buried, outdated, or missing
  • You avoid sharing the link because you do not like how it looks
  • Adding a new service or page feels painful
  • The design no longer matches the quality of your actual work

A redesign is not about following a trend. It is about removing friction between a potential customer and the decision to contact you.

What we keep, what we rebuild

Redesigns go wrong when everything is thrown away and replaced with something prettier but less useful. We start with an audit of your current site: which pages bring traffic, which messages land, which forms actually produce enquiries, and what people struggle with.

Keep

Working content, proven service descriptions, valuable pages, existing SEO equity, and any brand elements that still feel right.

Rebuild

Outdated layout, slow pages, confusing navigation, weak mobile experience, and calls to action that are easy to miss.

How the redesign works

  1. Audit. We review your site, content, speed, mobile experience, and basic search setup.
  2. Plan. We agree the page list, the priority messages, and what will be kept, rewritten, or removed.
  3. Design. You see a first direction in days, not weeks, and we refine it together.
  4. Build. The approved design becomes a fast, responsive site with clean markup.
  5. Launch. We handle redirects where needed, connect the domain, check forms and analytics, and hand over everything.

Questions, answered

Will I lose my existing search rankings?

A carefully managed redesign should protect the pages and signals that already work. We keep important URLs or add redirects, preserve titles and content where they earn traffic, and keep the technical foundations clean. Rankings can still shift, so we plan the migration rather than guessing.

Can you keep parts of my current site?

Yes. We start with an audit and keep the content, structure, and brand elements that are doing their job. The goal is a better site, not a different site for the sake of it.

Do I need new content and photos?

Not necessarily. We can work with what you have, tighten the copy, and improve the layout. If your photos are dated or low quality, we will suggest practical replacements or sourcing options.
