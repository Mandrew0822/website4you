A website that works as hard as you do

Most customers check you out online before they ever call, email, or walk through the door. If your site looks dated, loads slowly, or leaves basic questions unanswered, you lose that customer to someone who looks more established — even when your work is better.

A custom small business website fixes that. It gives you a professional home base you can put on a card, a van, a receipt, or a social profile. It explains what you do, shows proof, and makes the next step obvious. And unlike a social page, nobody can change the rules on you.

We build every site around three questions: Who are you talking to? What do they need to believe before they buy? And what should they do next? The answers shape the layout, the copy, and the calls to action.

Built for how customers search

Good design and good search visibility are not opposites. The technical basics are part of every build:

  • One clear topic per page, with a focused title and description
  • Clean heading structure that search engines can understand
  • Structured data that describes your business and services
  • Fast, lightweight pages with properly sized images
  • A sitemap and robots file so crawlers can find every page
  • Mobile-friendly layouts, since most local searches happen on a phone

We do not promise a number-one ranking. Nobody honest can. What we can do is remove the technical reasons a good site fails to get found, and give you a strong foundation to build content and reviews on top of.

Questions, answered

How much does a small business website cost?

A custom one-page site starts at $100 and a multi-page business site starts at $200. Every quote is fixed and agreed in writing before work begins, so the price will not creep up midway through.

Do you write the content for my business website?

We can. Send rough notes about what you do, who you serve, and what makes you different, and we will shape them into clear, confident website copy that fits the design.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site ships with the technical foundations search engines need: clean semantic markup, unique page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast loading, and mobile-friendly layouts. Rankings also depend on your content, competition, and ongoing work, so we never promise a specific position.

Can I update the website myself after launch?

Yes. We hand over the files and can either show you how to make simple text and image changes yourself or handle small updates for you on request.
